**Description:**
Changes is a free flashcard app for learning jazz harmony.

PRACTICE FOUR WAYS
• Spell it — see a chord symbol, name every note
• Name it — read a chord on the staff and keyboard, pick the symbol
• Hear it — ear training: identify chords by sound
• Progressions — work out ii–V–Is, turnarounds, blues and more from Roman numerals, in any key

EVERY CHORD A JAZZ PLAYER MEETS
81 chord types across 9 families: major, minor, dominant, altered, suspended, diminished and half-diminished, augmented, slash/hybrid chords and upper structures — with inversions, correct spelling in every key, and the scales that fit each chord.

LEARN AS YOU GO
An illustrated guide explains what each chord family is, how it sounds and how it's written. A full chord library lets you see, hear and study any chord in any key.

BUILT FOR PRACTICE
• Spaced repetition brings back the chords you miss
• Choose keys, chord families and how often inversions appear
• Works offline
• Light and dark themes
• No account, no ads, no tracking — completely free
